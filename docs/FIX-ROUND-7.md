# Draft House fix round 7: parcels, flood, zoning and the sourced rules table

Branch `claude/fix-round-7`, from `claude/fix-round-6`. Pictures and tables in `docs/fix-round-7/`.

One sentence: the Lot step now asks the county appraisal district for the parcel ("Is this your lot?"), reads the FEMA flood zone inside that parcel (and says plainly when there is no digital flood map), reads the city zoning district, drafts to a rules table where every number names its source, its status and its date, and shows the homeowner and the drafter the rules it used.

**Security lines kept as written.** No token is committed or logged (`REGRID_API_TOKEN`, `ASCE_HAZARD_API_KEY` are `sync: false` in `render.yaml`, the Regrid call is the only place the token travels, the wind module never logs the URL). Owner names and mailing addresses are drafter-only: `parcelView()` strips `owner`, `mailing` and the raw `attributes` for everyone else, candidates included, with a test over HTTP. CI never hits the network: the parcel, flood and zoning services answer from `test/fixtures/parcels/*.json` through `server/fixtures.mjs` (the tests pass the same `fetchImpl`; `DRAFTHOUSE_FIXTURES=1` does it for the running server); `scripts/make-parcel-fixtures.mjs` records them behind `LIVE=1` and redacts the owner and mailing fields before writing, and a test reads every committed fixture to prove no owner value survived. Nothing was deleted from Render.

**Said up front.** The sandbox this was built in cannot reach `gis.rgv911.org`, `cobgis.brownsvilletx.gov`, `services*.arcgis.com`, `hazards.fema.gov`'s NFHL or the city zoning servers (every one answers HTTP 000 through the proxy), so the committed fixtures are **synthetic**: built from the numbers the live lookups gave on Nov 17 (prop id 255056 / geo id P2500-00-004-0007-00 / PALMS LOT 7 BLK 4 / 10,428 sf for 1201 Kerria; five edges and 8,123 sf for 36 Melissa with 1021 Clarissa as the nearer neighbour; 9,479 sf and a 0.6 ft sliver for 5435 Curtis Clark) with stand-in coordinates, and marked `synthetic: true` with the reason in each file. Run `LIVE=1 node scripts/make-parcel-fixtures.mjs` once from the office and commit the recordings; the tests do not change.

## What the client sees, step by step

1. Address typed → "Looking the lot up at the county appraisal district…" → the card with the legal description, the four sides, the area, the source and date.
2. **Yes, this is my lot** → the lot draws from the parcel's calls, the setbacks resolve on its own width and depth, the flood zone is read inside it, the zoning district shows, the rules panel lists every number with where it came from. No checkbox (the client said yes); the drafter still confirms before the plan is sent on.
3. **Not my lot** or several candidates → the usual typed lot or the plat; the drafter pulls the parcel later.
4. On Send: the same panel, and a lot zoned for anything but a house blocks the send with "This lot is zoned C-3 (commercial). A house may need a rezoning; ask the office to call you." and the button.
5. The drafter: the inbox lists the parcel lot under "Lots to confirm", the card shows ids, source, fetched date, how it matched and the calls; the panel shows every source; the owner of record is a click away and never in the client app.

## Not met, and why

- **Live recordings.** The appraisal-district, FEMA NFHL and city zoning servers are unreachable from this build machine, so the fixtures are synthetic (marked, built from the Nov 17 numbers). The code paths, field names, headers and fallbacks follow the live research; a `LIVE=1` run from the office records the real answers into the same files and the tests stay the same.
- **Wind speed numbers.** By decision none are entered; every row says "set by the engineer of record" until a drafter fills it.
- **Rows marked unknown or summary.** San Juan, Mercedes, San Benito, Rio Grande City, Alice and the county rows carry unknowns; Mission, Pharr, Weslaco, Brownsville, Corpus Christi and others are summaries. The panel and the lot flags say so; the office dashboard is where a drafter turns them into code-read values with a source line.
- **PostGIS mirror.** Planned and scripted, not loaded or scheduled (item 12 waits for Rolando).
- **Regrid.** Dormant by design; the health page shows the failure rate that would justify turning it on.
